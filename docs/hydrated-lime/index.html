Bad lime costs you money. Here's how we prevent it.

Most lime problems show up in your plant, not on the invoice. These are the five we hear about most often.

Purity lower than promised

You pay for 90 % and dose for 80 %, so consumption goes up.

The grade is a guaranteed minimum

The grade number is the minimum Ca(OH)2 content, and every lot ships with a Certificate of Analysis.

Grit and silica

Inert matter wears pumps and dosing lines and adds sludge.

Silica capped for every grade

Acid insoluble is held to 1.06 % in the 92 grade and capped for every grade, and fineness is up to 98.5 % through 200 mesh.

Lumps and moisture

Damp lime cakes, blocks feeders and loses strength.

Dry powder, sealed bags

Moisture is limited to 0.5 to 2.0 % by grade, and the packing date is printed on every lot.

Every truck is different

One good lot, then the next one upsets your process.

Supplied to your approved sample

We agree the specification against the sample you approved, and every lot is traceable by lot number.

Storage and safe handling

Keep it dry and sealed

Store in a cool, dry place in the sealed original bags. Stack on pallets, away from walls.

Use within 6 months

Lime slowly absorbs CO2 and moisture from air, which lowers its effective grade.

Handle with care

Alkaline: irritates skin, eyes and lungs. Wear gloves, goggles and a dust mask. Rinse any contact with plenty of water.

Questions buyers ask

Which grade do I need?

Find your industry above and start with the grade shown in red. Then tell us your process: we will suggest a grade and send a sample with its Certificate of Analysis so you can test it.

Is the grade number a minimum or an average?

A minimum. The grade number is the minimum Ca(OH)2 content, and every lot ships with a CoA showing the measured result.

What is the difference between quicklime and hydrated lime?

Quicklime is calcium oxide (CaO). Hydrated lime is quicklime slaked with measured water to form dry calcium hydroxide, Ca(OH)2. It is safer to handle and easier to dose.

Do you export?

Yes. We pack in sealed, laminated bags and load containers by a fixed, checked method with a moisture barrier and a numbered seal. Send your port and monthly volume with your enquiry.

How long can I store hydrated lime?

Use it within 6 months. Lime slowly absorbs CO2 and moisture from the air, which lowers its effective grade. Keep it dry, in the sealed original bags, on pallets away from walls.
