How we make it

  • Our hydrated lime is purified to a clean calcium solution and filtered to 1 µm.
  • Food-grade phosphoric acid is added at pH 9 to 10, and hydroxyapatite precipitates.
  • The precipitate is aged and washed until the wash water is below 100 µS/cm.
  • It is dried at 105 °C, milled and sieved to size.

The reaction

10 Ca(OH)2 + 6 H3PO4 → Ca10(PO4)6(OH)2 + 18 H2O

Phase purity is confirmed by X-ray diffraction.

Questions buyers ask

Is your hydroxyapatite animal-free?

Yes. It is made synthetically from calcium hydroxide and food-grade phosphoric acid. No bone or fish is used.

What is the difference between micro and nano hydroxyapatite?

Nano hydroxyapatite has particles under 100 nm. Our micro grade (D50 3 to 8 µm) is available for samples now. Nano grade (20 to 100 nm) is in development.

What does the Ca/P ratio tell me?

Pure hydroxyapatite has a calcium to phosphorus molar ratio of 1.67. A ratio close to this shows the product is mostly hydroxyapatite.

Can I use it in a medical device or bone graft?

No. Medical-grade hydroxyapatite needs a medical device licence, and we do not offer it.
