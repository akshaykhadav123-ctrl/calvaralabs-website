Questions buyers ask

What is the difference between FOB, CFR and CIF?

FOB: we deliver the lime loaded on the ship at the Indian port; you pay sea freight and insurance. CFR: we also pay sea freight to your port. CIF: CFR plus marine insurance. Ex-works means you collect from our plant in Jodhpur.

Palletised or non-palletised: which should I choose?

Palletised bags are faster and cleaner to unload with a forklift and suffer less handling damage. Non-palletised (loose-loaded) bags fit more tonnes in each container, so the freight cost per tonne is lower. Ask Akshay to quote both.

How quickly will I get a price?

Usually the same day. Send your grade, packing, pallets, price basis, port and quantity, or use the quote form on this page.

Do you supply small trial orders?

Yes. Start with a free sample, then a trial lot, then regular supply. Ask Akshay for the trial quantity that suits your process.

Can you match our specification?

Send your specification or standard. We confirm the grade that meets it and send a sample with its CoA so you can test before ordering.

Tell us your port and quantity

Send the grade, packing and destination. You get price, container quantity, lead time and terms in one reply.
