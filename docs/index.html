How we make your hydrated lime

Five steps at our plant in Ransigaon, Jodhpur. Scroll through them to see what happens at each step and what it means for the lime you receive.

01

Quicklime from the kiln

Limestone from the Jodhpur limestone belt is burnt to quicklime (CaO) and carried by conveyor to the hydrator.

What it means for youGood stone and a full burn set the ceiling for purity. Every grade starts here.

02

Measured water

Water is added in a measured amount: just enough to slake the quicklime into a dry powder, no more.

What it means for youToo little water leaves quicklime that heats up in your process; too much makes lumps. Moisture stays between 0.8 and 2.0 %.

03

Hydrator

Quicklime and water are mixed as they react. The reaction gives off heat and steam and forms calcium hydroxide.

What it means for youCaO + H2O → Ca(OH)2 + heat. Fully hydrated lime is stable, safe to handle and easy to dose.

04

Classifier

Fine powder is carried on, while grit and coarse, silica-rich particles are separated out.

What it means for youThis sets each grade's fineness (up to 98.5 % through 200 mesh) and keeps silica down to 1.06 % in grade 92.

05

Packed and tested

The powder is filled in 25, 30, 35 or 40 kg bags or 900 kg jumbo bags. Every lot is tested in our plant lab.

What it means for youYou get the grade you approved, lot after lot, with a Certificate of Analysis on every dispatch.
